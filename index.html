Important Notice

GPS Mitra Location Changer uses Android's mock location functionality. Users must manually enable Developer Options and select GPS Mitra Location Changer as their mock location app. GPS Mitra Location Changer cannot automatically enable these system settings.

01 Introduction

Welcome to GPS Mitra Location Changer, a mock GPS location utility application developed by Pawan Kumar. This Privacy Policy explains how the application accesses and uses information when you use its features.

By using GPS Mitra Location Changer, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02 Information We Collect

GPS Mitra Location Changer does not require users to create an account or sign in. The app does not operate a developer-managed account system or backend server for collecting users' personal information.

The app may access location information and information needed to provide its selected mock location and map features. The selected mock coordinates are provided to Android's mock location system on the device.

We do not intentionally transmit users' selected mock coordinates to a developer-operated server.

03 Permissions Used

Depending on the features you use and your Android version, GPS Mitra Location Changer may request the following permissions:

● Precise Location
● Approximate Location
● Internet Access
● Network State

Permissions are controlled by Android and the user. You can review or revoke applicable permissions at any time through your device's Settings.

The app's actual permission requirements may vary depending on Android version and the features being used.

04 Location Services and Mock GPS

GPS Mitra Location Changer allows users to select a location and provide simulated GPS coordinates through Android's mock location framework.

The availability and behavior of simulated locations may depend on Android settings, device configuration and whether other applications accept mock location data. GPS Mitra Location Changer does not guarantee that every third-party application will use or display a simulated location.

Mock location functionality is controlled by Android's Developer Options and the user's device settings.

05 Background Operation

When the user starts mock location functionality, its operation may continue while GPS Mitra Location Changer is in the background, subject to the app's implementation, Android restrictions, device settings and system process management.

Users can stop the mock location functionality using the STOP control in the application. Removing the app from Recent Apps may also initiate cleanup, subject to Android's process lifecycle behavior.

06 Maps and Third-Party Services

GPS Mitra Location Changer uses OpenStreetMap-based map functionality and Nominatim place search services to display maps and help users find locations.

When you load map content or search for a place, your device may send requests to these third-party services. Such requests may expose technical information including your IP address, search queries, requested map areas and request details to the respective service providers.

Please review their policies:

  • OpenStreetMap
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy
  • Nominatim Usage Policy

These third-party services operate independently. Their data handling practices are governed by their own policies.

07 Data Storage and Sharing

GPS Mitra Location Changer does not currently operate its own server or user account database. App settings and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Map and place-search requests may be processed by the third-party services described in this policy. We do not sell users' personal information.

08 Advertising

GPS Mitra Location Changer does not currently display advertisements. If advertising services such as Google AdMob are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant advertising and data practices.

09 Data Security

We aim to minimize the information handled by GPS Mitra Location Changer. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their devices and Android software updated.

10 Children's Privacy

GPS Mitra Location Changer is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account system or backend server.

11 Data Retention and Deletion

GPS Mitra Location Changer does not maintain user accounts or a developer-operated database of users' personal information. Locally stored app settings can generally be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the application. Third-party services may retain request information according to their own policies.

12 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, services or data practices change. Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
